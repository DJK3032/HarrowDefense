PYLON is a body-worn node that puts every soldier, and the sensors they already wear, onto the tactical picture. Quiet by design. Off the air at the flip of a switch.

See PYLON Talk to us

Today the picture stops at the squad leader.

Below the leader, a soldier’s position is a voice report and their sensor data never leaves the body. Commanders and autonomous systems can only work for the soldiers they can see.

PYLON brings the rest of the squad onto the network.

Every soldier on the map

An individual position for each soldier, delivered straight into TAK. No phone or tablet on the body.

Sensors ride along

Readings from the wearables a soldier already carries travel with their position, so the leader sees condition as well as location.

Quiet on command

Emission control is enforced in hardware. One glove-operable switch takes the node off the air.

Built for anyone who works where the network isn’t.

The dismounted soldier comes first. The same node serves every team that needs to know where its people are and what condition they are in.
